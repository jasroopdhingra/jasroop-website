About

I write software in New York at Hanover Park, which does fund administration for venture firms, with AI doing the first pass and accountants checking it. Before that I was a founding engineer at Margin, an AI billing copilot for fulfillment companies, where I worked on the client dashboard and was the main technical contact for clients. Earlier I was on the founding team at Shapes, a consumer app for hanging out with people and AIs together, where I wrote all of the developer docs and ran office hours for our creator community. At Pieces I built Vim and Neovim plugins, and at Boston College I studied computer science and taught in the campus makerspace. On my own I've built a webcam program that draws a red border when I slouch, a decision-tree explorer for Oscar Health's coverage guidelines, and a dependency graph of Composio's tools.

A week of recruiting at Shapes left me with a firm opinion: I'd rather hire someone from a small company that failed than someone from a big one, because early stage is hard and you can't route around it. Running office hours taught me that nobody reads documentation, and that putting users on the spot gets you more honest answers. Goodreads has me at 130 books, and right now I'm partway through Sally Rooney's Intermezzo. I've been to 28 countries, from Reykjavik to Tokyo, and I live in New York. If any of this sounds interesting, reach me on X, LinkedIn, or GitHub.
