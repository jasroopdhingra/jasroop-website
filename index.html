About

I write software in New York at Hanover Park, which does fund administration for venture firms, with AI doing the first pass and accountants checking it. Earlier I was on the founding team at Shapes, a consumer app for hanging out with people and AIs together. At Pieces I built Vim and Neovim plugins, and at Boston College I studied computer science and taught in the campus makerspace.

Most of my free time goes to books. I'm somewhere past 130 of them now, currently in the middle of Sally Rooney's Intermezzo. I've been to 28 countries, from Reykjavik to Tokyo, and I live in New York. If any of this sounds interesting, reach me on X, LinkedIn, or GitHub.
