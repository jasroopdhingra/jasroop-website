Building a developer ecosystem

June 8, 2024

My first week at Shapes I did developer relations. I shipped some dashboard fixes, rewrote the docs, and answered a pile of questions. I wrote the Shapes dev docs.

What I actually learned:

  1. Office hours are the best way to get a straight answer. People are more honest when they have to say it out loud.
  2. Almost nobody reads the documentation.
  3. A lot of people using Shapes didn't know shapes.inc was a website. They found it through a friend or the Discord. They wanted their own shape. Plenty of them never got around to it.
