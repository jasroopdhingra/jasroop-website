Gaming and prompting

July 9, 2024

I play Call of Duty, and I write prompts. They feel the same in a dull way. You try a route. It fails. You try another. You change a sentence. The model does something else. You change it again.

A lot of sessions just end badly. That's most of the time. Sometimes it works and you stop touching it.
