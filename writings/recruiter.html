Being a recruiter for a week

June 4, 2024

For a few days in June 2024 I recruited staff engineers for Shapes. It was a seed-stage company. The work was scrolling profiles. It got repetitive fast.

I kept sorting people into three piles. At least the ones in San Francisco.

  1. Big tech. School was Berkeley, Stanford, or UCLA, then Google or Meta or somewhere like that.
  2. Startup jumper. A year or two, then the next company.
  3. Someone who joined very early, or started the thing.

The big tech profiles blur together. I think those are the jobs a model takes first. The other two are more interesting, because you can see what they were willing to bet on.

I'd rather hire someone from a small company that failed than someone from a big company or a late-stage startup. Early stage is hard. You can't really route around that.
