Twenty-eight countries

October 2026

I wrote down the countries I've been to and picked one city for each, so I could put them on a globe. Twenty-eight, counting the United States. I live in New York. The lines start there.

Europe is most of the list. London, Edinburgh, Dublin, Paris, Amsterdam, Brussels, Berlin, Prague, Vienna, Zurich, Rome, Vatican City, Lisbon, Madrid, Oslo, Reykjavik.

Closer to home: Toronto, Mexico City, Punta Cana, Kingston, Nassau.

Farther out: Istanbul, Dubai, New Delhi, Bangkok, Singapore, Tokyo.

I don't have a story for each stop. The list is what I have. The travels page draws an arc from New York to every other pin.
